## Players by role

**Superseded.** The v0 composite shown in this section had real methodological gaps
surfaced by an eye-test review against real lolesports knowledge: a region-bucketing bug
that mislabeled legitimate LCS players (LTA/LTA N tag) as "OTHER", no champion-context
normalization (rewarded playing carry champs regardless of skill), no explicit
region-strength weighting (let random LCS/LEC players outrank LCK/LPL stars), and
too-slow recency (120-day half-life diluted current form). Rewritten as
`scripts/ml/build_player_ratings.py` (v0.2) — see `docs/nucky_player_ratings_preview.md`
for the corrected rankings and `docs/nucky_v2.md`'s build log for details.
